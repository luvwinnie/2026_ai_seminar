I'll pull the confidence, criteria, privacy, and production pages next.The highest-impact fix is to stop gating Choice questions on `confidence` alone. On a 12-way phenomenon question, `confidence` 0.7 is only about a 0.73 top-class probability, and that number ignores how close the runner-up is.

## What already matches the docs

Splitting description-only and cause-only state, keeping thresholds in code, escalating low confidence, using an LLM only to draft CAPA (corrective and preventive action), and pausing LangGraph for approval are the pattern TypeSafe and LangChain describe: code owns the workflow, Jev only makes narrow judgments, and an LLM is the exception path. Sources: [How to build with TypeSafe](https://docs.typesafe.ai/concepts/how-to-build-with-system-one) (2026-09-20), [Building Prod with Jev and LangGraph](https://www.langchain.com/blog/building-prod-with-jev-and-langgraph) (2026-09-25).

## Ranked changes

### 1. Gate on the distribution, not on `confidence` (highest)

**A.** `confidence` is a summary of the probability shape, not a second opinion. TypeSafe says you may replace it, which is why the full `probabilities` map is returned. The docs’ own three-option demo is `(3 × largest probability − 1) / 2`. [Confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence) (2026-09-20). Noul has no `confidence`; the yes-probability is the whole distribution. [Noul](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/noul) (2026-09-20). A Score `score` is the probability-weighted level index; `jev-1.13` score levels are weak at numerical calibration, so do not treat a fractional severity as a precise magnitude. [Jev 1.13 jaggedness](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) (reviewed 2026-09-17).

**B.** On 738,164 Choice answers, reported confidence matches `(N × p_max − 1) / (N − 1)` to rounding error. It does not look at the second-place probability. [Is Jev confident?](https://logentropy.com/articles/is-jev-confident) (2026-09-18); same formula restated by [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/23/a-coding-guide-to-typesafe-ai-jev/) (2026-09-24).

**C.** Your bars are not comparable. Phenomenon `confidence` 0.7 with 12 options implies `p_max ≈ 0.725`. Factor `confidence` 0.5 with 10 options implies `p_max ≈ 0.55`. A factor split of 0.55 / 0.40 still clears 0.5. Auto-accept only when top-class probability and top-two margin both clear question-specific bars, and abstain otherwise. TypeSafe’s hierarchy cookbook treats `top / second` as a separation metric, not as something the model applies. [Hierarchical classification](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/hierarchical_classification) (2026-09-17).

### 2. One cost-sensitive band per question, refit on a holdout (highest)

**A.** Thresholds scale with the cost of a wrong action. Documented starting points are a 0.5 floor and about 0.85–0.9 before a destructive action; they are not settings. [Confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence) (2026-09-20), [Confidence-gated routing](https://docs.typesafe.ai/patterns/confidence-routing) (2026-09-20). For a Noul, 0.5 means yes and no are about equally likely. Raise the cut when a false yes is expensive; lower it when a missed yes is expensive; send the middle to a person. [Noul](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/noul) (2026-09-20). The Noul cookbook uses an illustrative band of no below 0.30, human from 0.30 through 0.70, yes above 0.70, with no second API call. [Self-consistency: nouls](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/consistency_noul_cookbook) (2026-09-22). Do not copy a Noul cut onto a Choice. [Jaggedness](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) (2026-09-17). Pin `jev-1.13.0`; `jev-latest` can move under a tuned threshold. Log the `model` field that comes back. [Models](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models) (2026-09-22).

**B.** The same model was calibrated on CLINC150 (ECE 0.020) and overconfident on Banking77 (ECE 0.094). [ASSAY-001](https://github.com/jourdanlabs/assay-001) (protocol frozen 2026-09-17). A support-routing ECE of 0.075 collapsed on random 3-SAT. [willkelly/jev-evaluation](https://github.com/WillKelly/jev-evaluation) (accessed 2026-09-27). On one sentiment set, isotonic regression cut ECE from 0.117 to 0.008; Platt only reached 0.052. [AnthusAI/Jev-Calibration](https://github.com/AnthusAI/Jev-Calibration) (accessed 2026-09-27). Arize’s Jev-as-judge write-up tunes the cut on labeled data and reports false positives and false negatives on a separate set, using balanced accuracy because a majority class inflates raw accuracy. [Arize](https://arize.com/blog/jev-as-a-judge/) (2026-09-23). Conformal risk on CLINC150 floored at 1.95% because 56.4% of answers were confidence exactly 1.0, including nine wrong. Summary in [awesome-jev-robustness](https://github.com/Yifan-Lan/awesome-jev-robustness) (snapshot 2026-09-26). I did not open that study’s raw file.

**Not found.** TypeSafe docs do not mention conformal prediction, Platt scaling, isotonic regression, or per-class cuts. No published Japanese manufacturing evaluation was found.

**C.** With 117 cases and 12 phenomenon classes, per-class cuts and conformal quantiles will overfit. Keep one operating point per question, choose it for the costly error (especially customer outflow), and report automation rate and error rate on cases not used to pick the cut. If the missing-info Noul is high, do not auto-accept phenomenon or factor even when their margin looks clean: low confidence often means the state is insufficient. [Confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence) (2026-09-20). For severity, threshold level probabilities (for example, require most of the mass on “critical”) rather than a fractional score.

English is the best-supported language; CJK is accepted but weaker, and confidence should be watched. [Models](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models) (2026-09-22). Measure the bands on Japanese reports. Do not assume an English reliability diagram transfers.

### 3. Rewrite criteria as boundaries, with an explicit “other” (high)

**A.** `jev-1.13` answers the words written, not the intent. Put the boundary in `instructions` and `criteria`. [Jaggedness](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) (2026-09-17). When two options are confused, use the same fields on each: what it covers, what it is not, and a few examples. Those field names are not reserved. [Choice](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/choice) (2026-09-20), [How to build](https://docs.typesafe.ai/concepts/how-to-build-with-system-one) (2026-09-20). Add `other` or “none of the above” when the list can miss. [Choice](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/choice) (2026-09-20). Prefer a structured state and name the fields in the question. [State](https://docs.typesafe.ai/concepts/state) (2026-09-20). Irrelevant text in state lowers accuracy. [Jaggedness](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) (2026-09-17). Questions that share a state can stay in one call: in TypeSafe’s parallel-question study, one batched call matched separate calls. [Parallel questions](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/parallel_questions) (2026-09-22).

**B.** Adding `other` absorbed 0.09–0.79 of the probability and changed confidence, because `N` changed. Freeze the option list after tuning. [Is Jev confident?](https://logentropy.com/articles/is-jev-confident) (2026-09-18).

**C.** Give phenomenon, factor, and 4M (man, machine, material, method) an `other` that always goes to a human, described with Japanese boundary cases taken from disagreements, not from all 117 reports. Do not paste the labeled set into `state`. Keep the two-call split only when the omitted text is irrelevant. Cause, systemic-cause, and CAPA questions usually still need the defect description. There is no official few-shot channel beyond `examples` inside criteria and material placed in `state`.

### 4. Verify CAPA with several literal Nouls (high)

**A.** Indirect questions (“does this address the root cause?”) are a known weak spot. Name the fields and split the judgment. [Jaggedness](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) (2026-09-17). State is not treated as hostile: text that argues for its own label can move the answer. The same page. The extraction-verification cookbook frames the bad case as `true` and escalates if any check fires, instead of averaging them away. [SDE cascade](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/sde_cascade) (2026-09-22). Screen model output, not only input. [Guardrails](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/llm_guardrails) (2026-09-22).

**C.** Ask separate Nouls: the action names the stated cause; it changes the process rather than only reworking the part; it is concrete enough to execute; it does not contradict the defect record. Put the NCR (non-conformance report) in `state` and the draft beside it, and say the draft’s claim that it “fixes the cause” is not evidence. A false yes here approves a record, so use a high action cut and a review band. The LLM may draft. It should not be the decider on the cases Jev abstains.

### 7. Runtime: pin, retry, durable resume (medium)

**A.** On 429 or 529, retry with backoff. The official SDKs do this and honor `Retry-After`. Limits published for Jev 1.13 are 250,000 tokens/second and 1,200 requests/minute, and they can change. [API reference](https://docs.typesafe.ai/api) (2026-09-20), [Models](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models) (2026-09-22), [Python usage](https://docs.typesafe.ai/sdk/python/usage) (2026-09-26). LangGraph interrupts need a checkpointer and a thread id. Wrap side-effecting calls in tasks so a resume does not repeat them. Use synchronous durability if a crash must not lose the pre-approval state. [Interrupts](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/langgraph/interrupts) (2026-09-25), [Durable execution](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/langgraph/durable-execution) (2026-05-26).

**Not found.** No TypeSafe circuit-breaker API, and no server-side cache for identical states. Cookbook JSON caches are for reproducible notebooks, not production.

### 8. Do not ensemble by default (low)

**A.** Repeated Noul probabilities in the official study had a mean standard deviation of 0.0102. One borderline item still moved from 0.43 to 0.53. The recommended fix is a review band, not another sample. [Self-consistency: nouls](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/consistency_noul_cookbook) (2026-09-22). Choice agreement at a 0.60 cut was 99.2% on that cookbook’s single ambiguous post, with flips on 2 of 8 questions. [Choice self-consistency](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/consistency_choice_cookbook) (2026-09-22). LangChain saw Jev-as-a-judge scores barely move across 100 runs. [LangChain, 2026-09-25](https://www.langchain.com/blog/building-prod-with-jev-and-langgraph).

**C.** Repeat a call only when the top two probabilities are within a few hundredths and the decision is expensive. A client cache of identical pinned requests is a latency optimization, not a quality one, and must be invalidated when the model pin or criteria hash changes.

## Bottom line

Change the accept rule from `confidence ≥ 0.7 / 0.5` to question-specific cuts on top probability and top-two margin, with a human band for Nouls and for `other`. Rewrite the criteria with Japanese boundaries. Decompose the CAPA check. Log every distribution against the eventual human label and refit those cuts on new data. Leave ensembling, conformal sets, and per-class thresholds until the labeled set is much larger than 117.
